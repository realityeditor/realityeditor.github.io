@ahdeshpande
I updated the link on the webpage.

It takes around 2 minute to fully load the system on the Arduino Yun.

There is a little white LED on the Arduino Yun that starts to light up after around 2 minutes indicating that the WLAN is working.

After some more seconds it should start blinking every second.
This indicates that the open hybrid platform is working.

Do you see the white LED blinking when you power up your Arduino Yun?
