Download Starter App

Lego WeDo 2.0 & Philips Hue Starter App

The Starter App allows you to connect the Lego WeDo 2.0 or some Philips Hue Lights. If you just want to experiment with Logic Crafting (the AR programming tool) this is the right starting point. Download the App on to your computer and open it. The app lets you save and print AR-Markers that you can place next to your objects. These Markers allow you to use AR to interact with the Objects. A menu helps you to get started with your Lego Wedo 2.0 set and/or Philips Hue lights. Use the Reality Editor for iOS to interact with your objects.
